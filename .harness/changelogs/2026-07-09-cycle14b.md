## Why "partial trim" 而不是 "skip & warn"

User 揀咗 **partial-trim** over defer/aggressive-cut：

- **Defer-to-cycle-15**: 風險係 cycle 14 commit 614 KB 落地後，cycle 15 R5 refactor
  嘅 shrink 預算（同樣 4-5 KB）未必夠 cover 14 KB gap，要再多 cycle。
- **Aggressive-cut**: 10 KB shrink 會 cut 埋 `// v3.x inline markers`，將來 debug
  git-blame / 回溯 cycle 8-13 嘅 fix 會輸 context，長遠維護成本高。
- **Partial-trim** (chosen): 5.4 KB shrink + 7 T27 assertions 做 lock-in，restore
  < 600 KB budget。Cycle 15 R5 handleAnswer refactor 預期再加 1-2 KB saving，
  進一步加固 margin。
